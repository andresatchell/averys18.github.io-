Scouting Report

Offense · Discipline

Progression, 2024–2025

Avery's bat has translated across age groups and seasons. She opened Spring 2024 hitting .624 with a .714 OBP across 56 games, then spent Fall 2024 playing up a division — hitting .538 with a .923 slugging percentage in 14U play under Coach Tracy Bougere while still anchoring her 12U roster. By Spring 2025 she was slugging .984 with 6 home runs in just 46 games.

Power & Run Production

Of her 108 career hits, 40 went for extra bases — 16 doubles, 12 triples, 12 home runs. Her at-bats-per-home-run ratio dropped to a blistering 10.5 in Spring 2025, and she drove in a run roughly every 1.5 at-bats that same season (41 RBI in 46 games).

Plate Discipline

64 career walks against 38 strikeouts, with a walk-to-strikeout ratio that climbed from 0.75 in Fall 2024 to an elite 3.33 by Spring 2025 — a 344% swing in plate-discipline efficiency in a single year. Her OBP has never dropped below .571 in any season.

Makeup

A 5'10" righty who influences the game on both sides of the ball — a dominant batter willing to play every position except pitcher and catcher to help the team win.
